What it does

Every batch on one timeline

Kit wine, fresh grapes, fruit wine, mead, cider and perry. A day counter, the stage, and the next step, which can wait for a date or for a gravity reading.

A reading in two taps

A keypad made for gravity numbers. Temperature correction runs as you type, and ABV and attenuation update with every reading.

Calculators that know the batch

Open a calculator from a batch and it is already filled in with its volume, last gravity, pH and ABV. One tap writes the dose back into the log.

Warnings before the mistake

Sorbate with malolactic bacteria, sulfite near the legal limit, sweetening before stabilising. “Looks finished” and “may be stalled” come from your own readings.

The gravity curve

Every reading on one chart with the target finish band, additions and rackings marked. Smooth with years of batches.

A checklist before bottling

Gravity stable, malolactic done, stabilised, free sulfite, carbonation within your bottle’s limit. Worked out from the log, each line with its reason.
