Calculators for wine, mead and cider

The same formulas as the Demijohn app, with the source under every result. They run in your browser: nothing you type is sent or saved.

  • ABV from OG and FGStandard and alternate formula, temperature correction, step feeds.
  • How much sugar or honeyFor a target gravity or potential alcohol, with the new volume.
  • Hydrometer temperatureCorrect a reading taken warm or cold, 60 °F or 20 °C hydrometers.
  • Gravity scales and tableSG, Brix, Plato, Oechsle, Babo, Baumé, with potential alcohol.
  • Mead for beginnersHoney for your batch, nutrients in steps, finishing and bottling.

Which one first

  1. Read the hydrometer at the right temperature. If the sample is warmer or colder than your hydrometer’s calibration, correct it with the temperature calculator.
  2. Set the start. The sugar and honey calculator gives the amount for a target gravity or potential alcohol.
  3. Measure the end. The ABV calculator works from your OG and your latest reading, including sugar or honey you added on the way.
  4. Another scale on the label or in a book? The converter and table covers SG, Brix, Plato, Oechsle, Babo and Baumé.

Doses are estimates. Add in steps, test again, follow the product label. For personal use only.
